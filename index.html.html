A Quarter-Century of Culinary Greatness in Hyderabad

Headquartered in Hasthinapuram, Hyderabad, Sri Sai Caterers & Events was established over 25 years ago under the visionary guidance of founder S. Ravinder.

What began as a passion for authentic South and North Indian cuisines has evolved into one of Hyderabad's most dependable catering services. Whether serving an intimate private dinner for 20 guests or a grand wedding of 10,000+ members, we guarantee perfection in every plate.
